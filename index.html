What I do

Pick one, or combine them into a single visit. Every job gets a preview before you pay.

Aerial photos and film

Wide, high shots that show the whole property, its setting and the view, plus short edited films.

For sales, lets, hotels and estates

360 virtual tours

Aerial and ground 360s joined into a tour that works on any phone or computer, no app needed.

For marketing, planning and remote viewing

Progress records

The same views from the same spots on every visit, so you can see exactly what changed and when.

For builders, renovations and estates

Maps and boundaries

Stitched, straight-down maps of a site at around 3 cm per pixel, with boundaries and features marked.

For architects, planners and landowners

3D models and terrain

Measurable 3D models and ground height data built from overlapping drone photos.

For design, earthworks and drainage

Land and habitat surveys

Woodland, coastline and habitat mapping, with the data delivered ready for GIS.

For estates and environmental consultants

How it works

Simple, and nothing to pay until you've seen the results.

  1. Tell me about it

    Where it is, what you need and when. I'll come back with a price within a day or two.

  2. I plan and fly

    I check the weather, airspace and access, then fly and photograph on the day.

  3. You see a preview

    A selection of finished images within a few days, so you know what you're getting.

  4. Pay and download

    Once paid, you get the full set in high resolution, ready to use.
