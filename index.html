Questions

How do people view a 360 tour?

Through a normal web link. It opens in any browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no app or account. People drag or tilt their phone to look around and tap to move between rooms.

Can I put the tour on my own website?

Yes. You get a link to share and a short piece of code that places the tour on any web page.

What happens if the weather's bad?

I watch the forecast in the days before and we move the date if it won't give good results. Interior 360s can usually go ahead whatever the weather.

What do I get from a mapping job?

Usually a measured aerial map (GeoTIFF), boundary and area files (KML or shapefile) and a PDF you can print. Height data and 3D models are available if you need them. Everything opens in QGIS, ArcGIS or Google Earth.

How far do you travel?

Edinburgh, East Lothian and the Lothians week to week. Further afield, like the Mallaig job, by arrangement.
