What I do

Pick one, or mix them. Every job gets a free preview before you pay.

Aerial photos

High, wide shots that show the whole property, the plot and the view. Ready for listings, brochures and social media.

Good for: estate agents, holiday lets, hotels, golf courses

Full property record

Aerial photos, a 360 tour of the inside, interior photos and a short video. Everything you need to show a place off in one visit.

Good for: sales, lets, landlords with several homes

Build progress records

The same shots, from the same spots, on a regular visit. A clear record of how a build or renovation is going, without being on site.

Good for: builders, self-builders, owners renovating from a distance

Site maps and 3D models

A stitched, straight-down map of a plot, garden, field or small site, plus a rough 3D model. Measurable, and ready to drop into plans.

Good for: architects, garden designers, farms and estates

How it works

Simple, and no money up front for local jobs.

  1. Tell me about it

    Where it is, what you need and when. I'll reply with a price within a day or two.

  2. I shoot it

    I check the weather and the airspace, then fly and photograph on the day.

  3. You see a preview

    A selection of finished images within a few days, so you know what you're getting.

  4. Pay and download

    Once you've paid, you get the full set in high resolution, ready to use.

Hi, I'm Ewan.

I spent ten years as a commercial drone pilot, inspecting wind turbines, power lines and industrial sites and turning the footage into maps and data. Now I bring that same care to homes and land closer to home.
